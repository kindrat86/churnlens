FAQ

You Ask. We Answer.

Is this for sellers or buyers?
Buyers only. Period. ChurnLens is a buyer-side tool. We work exclusively for the acquirer — we have no relationship with the seller, no incentive to make a deal look good. Our only job is to find what the seller's deck left out.
What CSV format do you need?
Any raw subscription export. Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, Paddle, Braintree — we take them all. Don't clean it. Don't reformat it. Send it raw. If the file has subscription start dates, amounts, customer IDs, and status fields, we can work with it.
How is this different from Baremetrics or ChartMogul?
Those are seller-side dashboards — they show the seller what they want to see. ChurnLens is buyer-side forensics. We don't track ongoing metrics; we dissect one CSV to find what summary dashboards obscure. Different tool, different side of the table.
How fast do I get the report?
Single-report: 48 hours. Pro: 24 hours. Dealmaker: 12 hours. Each report is reviewed by a human analyst — we don't auto-generate and hope for the best.
What if the seller won't share the raw CSV?
That's a red flag in itself. Serious sellers share their data. If they won't, ask yourself what they're protecting. But we've found most sellers will provide the CSV once you frame it as standard due diligence — just like reviewing financials.
Do you store or share the CSV data?
Never. CSV files are deleted after the report is delivered. We do not retain, resell, aggregate, or benchmark against your data without explicit opt-in. Your deal data stays your deal data.
