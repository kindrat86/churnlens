Frequently Asked Questions

What is revenue quality scoring?

Revenue quality scoring analyzes MRR data, logo retention, and concentration metrics to produce a 0-100 score indicating the health of a SaaS company's revenue.

How does ChurnLens detect hidden churn?

ChurnLens identifies patterns in MRR decline, annual plan renewal rates, inactive paid accounts, and revenue concentration that indicate churn risk not visible in headline metrics.

What data do I need to use ChurnLens?

You need MRR data by customer, logo retention history, and plan information. The tool analyzes this data to surface acquisition risks.

Is ChurnLens only for acquisitions?

While built for due diligence, ChurnLens is also valuable for ongoing revenue health monitoring and board reporting.

How accurate is the revenue quality score?

The score is based on proven SaaS metrics frameworks. It cross-references multiple data points to minimize false positives and negatives.

How to use these FAQ answers in a live deal

The answers on this page are written for a buyer sitting in a data room with a revenue ledger open in one window and a deadline in the other. Each answer gives you the short version (the benchmark or definition a seller will quote) and the longer version (the definitional choices and failure modes that make the short version unreliable). The goal is to give you the knowledge to interrogate the seller's numbers, not just to accept them.

The highest-leverage use of this FAQ is to identify which metrics in the seller's data room are most likely to be massaged, then reconstruct each one from the raw revenue ledger under a consistent definition. ChurnLens automates that reconstruction; the FAQ gives you the context to interpret the results and the negotiating language to act on them.
