Best Churn Analytics Tools for SaaS Acquirers in 2026: Compared

A side-by-side look at the leading churn analytics and customer retention platforms, written for SaaS acquirers running pre-acquisition due diligence.

Not all churn analytics tools are built for the same reader. Some help operators run a subscription business day to day; others help acquirers evaluate one before purchase. This guide keeps those two readers separate: it describes what each platform is documented to do, who it is built for, and what it costs on the vendor's own pricing page, so you can decide what belongs in a diligence workflow.

What acquirers need from churn analytics

Before you write an LOI, you need answers to three questions that growth dashboards rarely foreground: (1) how much recurring revenue is concentrated on a handful of accounts, (2) whether net revenue retention is flattered by expansion that masks logo churn, and (3) how much annual-contract revenue is approaching a renewal cliff. Most tools in this comparison are designed for the operator watching their own book of business, not for a buyer stress-testing someone else's, and that difference shows up in the workflows each product documents.

  • Primary sources only. Every external claim links to the vendor's own product or pricing page, retrieved August 31, 2026. Review aggregators were not used.
  • What "Not publicly documented" means. Where a vendor does not publish a price, the table says so instead of estimating. It is a statement about the vendor's pricing page, not about the product's value.

  • Buyer vs operator. The comparison lens is pre-acquisition due diligence: reading a target's revenue history you did not generate. Tools built to help a team retain its own customers are described on their own terms, not penalized for a job they do not claim to do.

The tools in detail

ChartMogul

ChartMogul is a subscription analytics platform: it connects billing sources such as Stripe, Chargebee, and Paddle and computes MRR, churn, expansion, contraction, and cohort views for the business being measured. It is built for teams running their own subscription company, and its pricing scales with the ARR tracked in the platform. Choose it when you operate a SaaS business and want investor-grade metrics for your own data.

Baremetrics

Baremetrics monitors subscription revenue for subscription businesses, with dashboards for MRR, churn, upgrades, and downgrades, plus trial insights, cancellation insights, forecasting, and an optional payment-recovery add-on. Like ChartMogul, it is an operator tool: it watches the store you run. Choose it when you want live metrics and dunning for your own billing stack.

Paddle / ProfitWell

ProfitWell Metrics, now part of Paddle's merchant-of-record billing platform, provides free subscription revenue reporting for subscription and SaaS companies, alongside Retain for recovering failed payments. It is positioned at operators using or considering Paddle billing. Choose it when free MRR and churn dashboards for your own business are the job.

Gainsight

Gainsight is an enterprise customer success and retention platform: customer health scoring, journeys, AI agents, renewal forecasting, and post-sales workflows for CS organizations. Pricing is not publicly documented; access starts with a demo. Choose it when you run a CS team whose mandate is retaining and expanding an existing customer base.

ChurnZero

ChurnZero is customer success software built for proactive retention: health scores, journey builders, plays and automation, in-app communication, renewal forecasting, and AI agents that act between customer touchpoints. Pricing is not publicly documented; access starts with a demo. Choose it for the same operator-side job as Gainsight, typically in mid-market and enterprise CS teams.

ChurnLens

ChurnLens approaches churn from the other side of the table. It is built for acquirers, PE and M&A analysts, searchers, and sellers preparing for a sale: you send a target's subscription CSV exports and get a revenue-quality and churn-risk report covering logo retention, revenue concentration, annual-plan churn risk, inactive paid accounts, and MRR decline. The Starter tier (unlimited in-browser analyses) is free, Pro is $49/month or $499/year, Dealmaker is $199/month, and a one-time human-reviewed analysis costs $9. Choose it when the question is not "how do we reduce churn?" but "how much hidden churn are we buying?"

When to choose each

Choose ChurnLens if…

  • You are acquiring a SaaS business and need to price the deal on true revenue quality
  • You need a diligence-ready read you can hand to an investment committee
  • You care about concentration risk, retention quality, and annual-plan cliffs, the three risks growth dashboards understate

Choose an operator tool (ChartMogul, Baremetrics, ProfitWell) if…

  • You run a subscription business and need a live metrics dashboard for your own data
  • You need day-to-day churn tracking, dunning, or forecasting
  • You want turnkey integrations with your billing stack

Choose Gainsight or ChurnZero if…

  • You run a mid-market or enterprise CS team with proactive retention playbooks
  • Your problem is keeping customers, not evaluating them for purchase
Honest note: ChurnLens is not a replacement for these tools if you are the operator. It has no dunning, no live ops tooling, and is not designed to run a subscription business. It is a diligence lens. If you are the buyer, add ChurnLens to your stack; if you are the founder, keep what you have.

The verdict

Different jobs, different tools. If you are the operator, ChartMogul, Baremetrics, and ProfitWell report the churn of a business you run, and Gainsight and ChurnZero help you act on it. If you are the buyer, those tools' documented workflows assume you operate the business rather than evaluate it. ChurnLens exists for the second seat: it turns a target's own CSV exports into the concentration, retention-quality, and annual-cliff reads an IC memo needs. Start with the tool built for the seat you are sitting in.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both ChurnLens and the seller's existing analytics?

Yes. Most acquirers run ChurnLens alongside the seller's existing dashboard. The seller's tool tells the headline story; ChurnLens checks whether the story holds up under scrutiny.

Is ChurnLens a replacement for ProfitWell or Baremetrics?

No. Those tools are for operators running their own business. ChurnLens is for a buyer evaluating someone else's business for acquisition. They complement rather than compete.

Which churn analytics tool is the cheapest?

It depends on the seat. For operators, ProfitWell Metrics is free and ChartMogul has a free tier for companies under $10K MRR with paid tiers scaled to ARR, while Baremetrics starts at $75/mo. For acquirers, ChurnLens Starter is free and a one-time analysis is $9. Gainsight and ChurnZero do not publish pricing.

Does ChartMogul or Baremetrics do acquisition due diligence?

Not per their own product documentation. Both are subscription analytics platforms for operating your own business. For buyer-side diligence you would export the target's data and analyze it with a diligence tool.

How was the pricing on this page verified?

Every external price was read from the vendor's official pricing or product page on August 31, 2026. Where a vendor does not publish pricing, the table says Not publicly documented rather than guessing.
