What these tools can and cannot tell you

They compute from summary figures you supply, which makes them a fast first-pass screen. What they cannot see is anything that only emerges from customer-level data: cohort decay curves, renewal-cliff timing, or concentration in specific logos. That needs the full monthly MRR-by-customer ledger, which is what our paid analysis reads.

The scoring bands the tools apply are thresholds, not survey results. The sourced benchmark figures, with citations, are on the benchmarks page.
